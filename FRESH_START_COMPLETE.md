## 📝 Important Notes

### Card Placement Rules
- **+1 Rule:** Next card must be exactly 1 rank higher than target's top card
- **Examples:**
  - 5 → 4 (valid)
  - King → Queen (valid)
  - Ace → King (valid)
  - 2 → Ace (valid)

### Player Positions
- **Position 0** = You (bottom of screen)
- **Position 1** = Opponent (top of screen)
